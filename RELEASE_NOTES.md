## MasterClip for Android 1.0.0

Turn any YouTube or Twitch video into vertical Shorts, Reels and TikToks, right on your phone.

**Which file do I download?**
- **MasterClip-1.0.0-arm64-v8a.apk**: almost every phone from 2017 onwards. Pick this one if you're not sure.
- **MasterClip-1.0.0-armeabi-v7a.apk**: very old or very cheap phones, if the first one says "App not installed".
- **MasterClip-1.0.0-x86_64.apk**: emulators and Chromebooks.

**How to install**
1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
2. If your phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app.
3. Open MasterClip, paste a link (or share it from the YouTube app), and tap **Make clips**.

**What it does**
- Finds the most replayed moments on YouTube, or the loudest moments in other videos.
- Downloads only those parts, not the whole video.
- Makes each clip 9:16:
  - With a face cam, the cam goes on top and the game goes below.
  - With a person talking, the crop follows their face.
  - Otherwise, the whole video sits in the middle on a blurred background.
- Adds big word-by-word captions from YouTube's subtitles.
- Saves the clips to your Gallery (Movies › MasterClip), ready to share.
- Keeps working while your phone is locked.

No account and no sign-in. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
